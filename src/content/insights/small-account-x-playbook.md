Growing a brand account from almost nothing is a very different job from growing one that already has reach. With a small account, your posts barely get seen. Your replies are where the growth comes from.

## Replies are your distribution

When you have a few hundred followers, the biggest audiences you can reach are in other people's comment sections. A sharp, useful or funny reply under a big account can get more eyes than a week of your own posts.

Make a list of the accounts your future users already read. Show up there daily. Add something, don't just agree.

## Sound like the timeline

Crypto-native audiences can smell a marketing department from a mile away. Write the way people on your timeline write: short, direct, a bit of personality. Leave the corporate tone for the press kit.

## Make the profile do the selling

Every reply that lands sends people to your profile. When they get there:

- The bio should explain what you do in one line
- The pinned post should show the product, not a generic welcome
- The banner should reinforce the positioning

You get one look. Don't waste it.

## Post less, but better

Small accounts often try to post everything. Better to post fewer things that are actually good, and spend the rest of the time engaging.

## Use Spaces to borrow trust

Spaces put a voice behind the brand and let you show up in front of other communities. Co-hosting with adjacent projects is one of the fastest ways to reach people who already care about your category.

## Be patient with the curve

Small-account growth is slow, then sudden. Keep showing up. The compounding is real.
